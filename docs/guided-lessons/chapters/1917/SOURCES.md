## S2. Who could participate in the Constituent Congress?

Two contemporary documents in UNAM-hosted historical reprints:

- [Decree of 14 September 1916](https://archivos.juridicas.unam.mx/www/bjv/libros/6/2883/18.pdf),
  Article 4, printed pp.65–66 / PDF 3–4: candidate exclusion for specified assistance to hostile
  governments/factions.
- [Convocation of 19 September 1916](https://archivos.juridicas.unam.mx/www/bjv/libros/6/2883/19.pdf),
  Article 1, printed p.67 / PDF 1: elected constituent deputies, Querétaro, and December 1 opening.

**Status:** relevant text checked by source agent. No verbatim excerpt is used in the storyboard.
**Use:** explain the institutional setting and why this was not an all-factions reconciliation.
**Limit:** eligibility rules do not establish every delegate's social background, every motive,
or unanimity on social provisions. The statement about the assembly's political setting is an
editorial interpretation grounded in these rules, not a quotation from them. Do not equate
absence of a faction's leaders with absence of its demands.

**Reuse:** links and authored explanation; do not reproduce reprint page images without review.

## S3. Anenecuilco letter: a local need before 1917

**Historical creators / recipient:** residents of Anenecuilco, Morelos, addressing the state
governor. The study dates the letter to 25 April 1910; its reproduced ending says April 1910.
**Genre / language:** petition letter in Spanish, known here through a modern transcription.

**Inspected edition:** Tebaldo Mureddu Torres, *Breves comentarios al derecho de propiedad en
el Estado de Morelos. Su relación con la Revolución del Sur*, Suprema Corte de Justicia de la
Nación, 2008, printed p.109.

- [UNAM-hosted chapter](https://archivos.juridicas.unam.mx/www/bjv/libros/15/7102/8.pdf), PDF 15.
- [SCJN complete book](https://www.scjn.gob.mx/sites/default/files/publicaciones_scjn/publicacion/2017-03/71142.pdf),
  PDF 123.

**Checked:** the letter transcription describes an urgent request to sow maize before the
rains while ownership remained disputed. The storyboard's short fragment matches both retrieved
PDF text and the printed page image, visually checked by the primary agent. The translation is
newly prepared and labeled.

**Not checked:** the original manuscript, signatures, and archival call number. Web screenshot
retrieval failed, but a temporary local rendering of the institutional PDF enabled visual checking
of printed p.109. Do not label the 2008 page a 1910 manuscript facsimile or invent a named speaker.

**Limit:** evidence of a prior demand, not proof of a 1917 outcome or of direct influence on
Article 27. Do not import the study's broader rhetorical claims into the narration.
**Reuse:** book marked © SCJN 2008; no open page-image license verified. Link only for the page;
the nine-word A1 analytical quotation is covered by the specific decision below.
